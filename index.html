How Panel’d works

1. Choose a page from the class library, or upload your own page image. 2. Color the page. 3. Fill in sections A–E. 4. Export a PDF and turn it in.

A
Summary — Retell the chapter's key events in your own words: who, what, where, and why it matters.
B
Quote Analysis — Copy one important line exactly, cite it (Page #, Panel #), and explain what it reveals.
C
Literary Elements — Name one element (symbolism, irony, a close-up…), show where it appears, and explain what it does.
Pg
Page Study — Analyze the page you chose: panel sizes, framing, gutters, what's shown and what's left out.
D
Theme Analysis — State a theme as a full sentence (a message about life) and support it with evidence.
E
Color Study — Color parts of your page on purpose, then finish the sentence explaining why.

Counting panels: start at the top left and read like a sentence — left to right, then down. Panel 1 is the first box you read.
